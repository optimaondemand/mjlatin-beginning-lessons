📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Are you ready for the Module 1 Quiz?
No new rules today — just pulling the whole sound system together: vowels, diphthongs, consonants, syllables, and stress. Pronounce a set of Roman names the confident way, play the Ludus, and take a self-check.
📚 PART 1 · THE WEEK ON ONE PAGE
Vowels: short vs. long — the macron holds a vowel twice as long, and can change a word's meaning (malum vs. mālum).
Diphthongs: only ae, au, oe glide as one syllable.
Consonants: C = /k/, G = hard, V = /w/, J/I = /y/, doubles are held.
Stress: long penult → stress it; short penult → stress the antepenult.
📚 PART 2 · SAY IT THE ROMAN WAY

Read each name aloud, applying every rule from this week:

Gallia · Britannia · Graecia · Hispānia · Sicilia · Rōma
Say it out loud: GAL-lee-a · bri-TAN-nee-a · GRAI-kee-a · his-PAN-ee-a · si-CIL-ee-a · RO-ma.
Notice: hard C and G throughout, the ae diphthong in Graecia, held double consonants in Gallia and Britannia, and the penult rule deciding every stress mark.
📚 PART 3 · SELF-CHECK

If these feel easy, you're ready for the quiz. If not, replay the lesson or the Ludus first.
