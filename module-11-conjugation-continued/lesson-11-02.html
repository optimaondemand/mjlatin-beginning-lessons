🎵 MEMORY WORK
Your target is the complete six-form paradigm for amō, amāre (to love). Chant it aloud — singular row first, then plural:
PersonSingularPlural

📚 PART 1 · THE WHOLE PATTERN

Two questions — who? (the row) and how many? (the column) — are answered by a single ending. This is a paradigm: a complete, systematic table of a word's forms.

Singular (one)Plural (many)
1st personamō — I loveamāmus — we love
2nd personamās — you loveamātis — you all love
3rd personamat — he/she/it lovesamant — they love
💡 Learn it once, use it everywhere. This same six-form pattern works for every 1st-conjugation verb you know: laudō, portō, habitō, navigō, superō, laborō — and the new verbs you'll meet later this week. That's already eight verbs sharing one paradigm.
