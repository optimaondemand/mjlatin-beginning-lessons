📚 PART 1 · READING THE DICTIONARY FORM

Every Latin dictionary lists a noun two ways: the nominative singular, then a second ending. That second ending is a shortcut — it tells you the noun's declension before you've parsed a single sentence:

via, viae f.
ending in -ae → 1st declension
templum, templī n.
ending in -ī → 2nd declension

Before you can parse a single form, you need the dictionary entry. That habit — check the entry, know the declension — is what this whole week builds.
