📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Can you produce every S1 paradigm — noun and verb — without looking?
This is the big chant session. You've drilled esse and the fourteen 1st-conjugation verbs separately, module by module. Today you say them all, back to back, until they're automatic. This is exactly what Roman schoolchildren did in the ludus litterarius — and it's the single best thing you can do before the exam.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recite all six forms of esse and all six forms of a 1st-conjugation verb, in order, from memory.
2
I will be able to name all fourteen 1st-conjugation verbs you've learned and give an English meaning for each.
3
I will be able to identify the person and number of any verb form on sight.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED FOR THIS LESSON
▶
Your Latin notebook
▶
A quiet minute or two to say the chants out loud
