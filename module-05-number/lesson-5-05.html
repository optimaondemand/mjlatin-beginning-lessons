📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Are you ready for the Module 5 Quiz?
No new grammar today — just pulling the week together: the four-cell paradigm, the agreement rule, and the three little words. Read a short passage the Roman way, play the Ludus, and take a self-check.
📚 PART 1 · THE WEEK ON ONE PAGE
The paradigm — one pattern, every 1st-declension noun:
SingularPlural
Nominative — subject-a-ae
Accusative — object-am-ās
Agreement: one subject → est · many → sunt.
Little words: et (and) · sed (but) · non (not, before the verb).
📚 PART 2 · READ IT THE ROMAN WAY

Run the reader's checklist on this short passage. Find each subject, check its number, and confirm the verb agrees:

Insulae provinciae sunt. Portae et fossae sunt, sed copiae non sunt.
Read it out: “The islands are provinces. There are gates and ditches, but there are no troops.”
Every subject here is plural (-ae), so every verb is sunt — and non sits right before the last sunt to negate it.
📚 PART 3 · SELF-CHECK

If these feel easy, you're ready for the quiz. If not, replay the lesson or the Ludus first.
