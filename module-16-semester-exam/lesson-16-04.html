📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Can you handle every kind of question, back to back?
No new grammar today — this is the dress rehearsal. You'll move through the same six kinds of questions as the exam, one right after another, using fresh sentences you haven't seen before. This is exactly the rehearsal that makes the real thing feel routine.
🎵 MEMORY WORK · TWO RULES THAT DECIDE EVERYTHING
Agreement: singular subject → est · plural subject → sunt (or the matching 1st-conjugation person/number ending).
The neuter rule: for 2nd-declension neuter nouns, the nominative and accusative are always identical.
📚 PART 1 · PARADIGM + CASE PARSING

Identify the case, number, and job of each underlined word.
