📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Are you ready to take the Semester Exam?
No new grammar — just one last self-check, and then everything you need to know to walk into the exam calmly. You've spent fifteen modules building this. Today is about trusting it.
📚 PART 1 · FINAL SELF-CHECK

If these feel easy, you're ready. If not, that's useful information — loop back to Lesson 16.02 or 16.04 before you start the exam.

📝 PART 2 · TAKING THE SEMESTER EXAM
1
The Semester Exam is the capstone assessment for all of Semester 1. It's worth 15% of your overall course grade — its own category, separate from the 45% you've already earned across your regular Module Quizzes.
2
It's 40 points, fully auto-graded. You'll see your score the moment you submit.
3
It covers exactly what you toured in Lesson 16.01 and rehearsed in Lesson 16.04: paradigm identification, case parsing, verb forms, translation in both directions, and grammar concepts.
4
A score of 32/40 (80%) shows you've mastered Semester 1 and moves you into Semester 2. If you don't reach that on your first attempt, the retake is supportive, not punishing — review what you missed, and try again. There's no penalty for taking the time you need to get there.
5
There's no Ludus this module — the exam itself is this week's whole assessment.
🌟 After you finish: two optional activities are waiting if you'd like to keep going — composing three original Latin sentences with illustrations, or building a full memory-map of this semester's grammar system to carry into Semester 2. Neither is required; they're there for anyone who wants a creative way to consolidate everything you've learned.
🏆 ONE LAST WORD

Learning an inflected language takes real perseverance — holding several paradigms in memory at once, drilling forms until they stop feeling foreign, pushing through a sentence that doesn't make sense on the first read. You did that, module after module, for a whole semester. Whatever your score turns out to be, that effort is already real. Now go show what you've built.
