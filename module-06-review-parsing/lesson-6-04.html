📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
What order should you check things in?
You now know every piece: case, number, function, particles, prepositions. Today you get a fixed order to check them in — a checklist you can run on any sentence, even ones with two or three clauses joined by et or sed.
📚 PART 1 · THE FIVE-STEP PROTOCOL

When you meet a new sentence, work through these steps in order — every time, without skipping.

1
Find the verb. At this stage it will be a form of esse (est/sunt) or amat/amant.
2
Find the nominative noun(s). This is your subject — or a predicate nominative if the verb is esse.
3
Find the accusative noun(s). Decide: direct object, or object of a preposition (ad, per, trāns, ante, post)?
4
Spot any particles: et joining two subjects or objects, sed pivoting the sentence, non sitting right before the verb it negates.
5
Write out the complete parse — every noun labeled — before you attempt a translation.
💡 Why the verb first? Once you know whether the verb is a form of esse, you already know whether a nominative noun could be a predicate nominative at all. Skipping step 1 is the most common parsing mistake.
📚 PART 2 · A COMPOUND SENTENCE, STEP BY STEP

Watch the checklist run on a two-clause sentence:

Femina viam amat sed ad provinciam non est.
Step 1 — verbs: amat, est. Step 2 — nominative: femina (1st decl., nom. sg., subject — shared by both clauses). Step 3 — accusative: viam (1st decl., acc. sg., direct object of amat); provinciam (1st decl., acc. sg., object of the preposition ad). Step 4 — particles: sed pivots the sentence; non sits right before est, negating it. Step 5 — translation: “The woman loves the road, but she is not near the province.”
📚 PART 3 · RUN THE CHECKLIST YOURSELF

Use the five steps to answer each question about these harder sentences.

✅ CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING
Read each sentence carefully, then answer.
Puellae et feminae nautam amant. (The girls and the women love the sailor.) Which noun is joined to puellae by et?
Nauta viās et provinciās amat. What are viās and provinciās?
Copiae ad provinciam non sunt, sed ad Italiam sunt. What does non negate?
