📚 PART 2 · READ IT THE ROMAN WAY

Run the full parsing checklist on this passage. Find every subject, every object, every verb, and every preposition phrase before you read the translation below:

Rōma magna est. Servī laborant et dominōs vocant. Nautae trāns provinciam navigant. Agricolae terrās amant et semper laborant. Puellae et feminae forum spectant. Dominus servōs bonōs laudat. Fossae et portae magnae sunt, sed copiae non sunt.
Read it out: “Rome is great. The slaves work and call the masters. The sailors sail across the province. The farmers love the lands and always work. The girls and women watch the forum. The master praises the good slaves. There are gates and large ditches, but there are no troops.”
Notice every noun type at work: 1st declension (Rōma, terrās, provinciam, puellae, feminae, fossae, portae, copiae), 2nd declension masculine (servī, dominōs, nautae is the 1st decl. masculine exception, dominus, servōs bonōs), 2nd declension neuter (forum, nom./acc. singular), esse (est, sunt), and 1st conjugation verbs (laborant, vocant, navigant, amant, spectant, laudat).
